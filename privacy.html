Privacy & Data Architecture

Privacy Policy

Our architectural philosophy is simple: minimize data retention, encrypt communication, and prioritize user privacy.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Omaglee handles information when you use our website, client interfaces, and real-time random video chat services.

2. Ephemeral Media & Peer-to-Peer Streams

Video chats are established as peer-to-peer (P2P) connections using WebRTC encryption. Audio and video streams flow directly between participants. Omaglee does not record, intercept, or store your video or audio conversations.

Ephemeral Sessions: Once a call ends or you skip to the next stranger, the connection is torn down immediately. No video logs exist on our servers.

3. Camera & Microphone Permissions

Your browser requests explicit hardware permission to access your camera and microphone. These permissions remain under your local control. You can mute your microphone or turn off your camera at any moment using the on-screen dock controls.

4. Relay-Only Privacy Mode

By default, WebRTC connections exchange direct IP addresses between peers to establish the lowest-latency connection. If you prefer not to expose your IP address to strangers, you can enable Relay-Only Privacy Mode in the Settings modal, which routes all media strictly through our secure TURN relay servers.

5. Safety Reports & Moderation Logs

When a user reports a violation, our server logs the anonymous participant session identifier, reported reason, and timestamp solely for investigating abuse and enforcing permanent platform bans.
