Safety & Standards

Community Guidelines

Our standards for keeping Omaglee safe, respectful, and enjoyable for everyone worldwide.

Core Principles

Omaglee is built on mutual respect and positive serendipity. Every time you connect with a stranger, remember there is a real human being on the other side of the screen.

Strictly Prohibited Behavior

Zero Tolerance: Violating any of these rules will result in immediate termination of the session and a permanent hardware/IP ban from Omaglee.
  • Nudity & Sexually Explicit Content: Showing nudity, engaging in sexual acts, or broadcasting adult content is strictly forbidden.
  • Underage Participation: Omaglee is an 18+ service. Minors are strictly prohibited. Attempting to exploit or contact minors will be reported to law enforcement authorities.
  • Harassment & Hate Speech: Bullying, racial or ethnic slurs, discrimination, threatening violence, or doxxing will not be tolerated.
  • Spam & Commercial Broadcasts: Using automated bots, pre-recorded promotional loops, or phishing scams is prohibited.
  • Recording Others: Capturing or distributing another user's video or voice without their explicit consent is a violation of their privacy.

Tools to Protect Yourself

You are always in control of your chat experience:

  • Skip (Next ⏩): If you feel uncomfortable or uninterested, click Next to move to another stranger instantly.
  • Report Abuse: Click the Report button in the dock or call overlay to submit a report directly to our moderation system.
  • Block Stranger: Blocking a stranger ensures our matchmaking queue will never pair you with that user again.
